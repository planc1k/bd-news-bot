# 📱 ScrollUp Today - Brand Guidelines

## 🎨 Brand Identity

**Brand Name:** ScrollUp Today  
**Instagram:** @scrl.up  
**Tagline:** "Quick, concise news updates. Follow us for latest headlines."  
**Category:** News & Media Website  

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

ScrollUp Today's design is characterized by:
- **Bold, Direct Communication** - Headlines that grab attention
- **Clean, Professional Layout** - Easy to read and understand
- **Strategic Color Use** - Red/orange highlights for emphasis
- **Photo-First Storytelling** - Real news images when available
- **Consistent Branding** - ScrollUp Today mark on every post

## 📐 Layout Styles

### Style 1: Text-Focused Posts
**Use When:** No news photo available or text is the focus

**Layout:**
```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│                         │
│   HEADLINE WITH         │
│   [RED HIGHLIGHT] ON    │
│   KEY PHRASES           │
│                         │
│                         │
│   Source: ...  Date: .. │
│   ScrollUp Today   ●    │
└─────────────────────────┘
```

**Characteristics:**
- White OR black background (alternates)
- 2-4 words highlighted in red boxes
- Large, bold typography
- Source + date at bottom
- Brand name bottom right

**Examples from Your Feed:**
- Railway operations post (black background)
- Any text-heavy announcements

### Style 2: Photo Posts
**Use When:** Strong news photo is available

**Layout:**
```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│ HEADLINE WITH           │
│ [RED HIGHLIGHT]         │
│                         │
│ Source: ..    Date: ..  │
├─────────────────────────┤
│                         │
│     NEWS PHOTO          │
│                         │
│          ScrollUp Today●│
└─────────────────────────┘
```

**Characteristics:**
- White background on top (45%)
- News photo on bottom (55%)
- Headline with red highlight
- Source/date above photo
- Brand on photo (white text)

**Examples from Your Feed:**
- Curfew announcement (soldiers photo)
- District violence post (street scene)

## 📝 Typography

### Headlines
- **Font:** Bold, Sans-serif
- **Size:** 50-70px
- **Color:** Black on white, White on black
- **Style:** All caps or title case
- **Highlight:** Red background boxes on key 2-4 words

### Body Text
- **Font:** Regular Sans-serif  
- **Size:** 28-36px
- **Usage:** Secondary information, captions

### Source/Date
- **Font:** Regular Sans-serif
- **Size:** 24-28px  
- **Color:** Gray (#646464) or slightly transparent
- **Format:** 
  - Left: "Source: Jago News 24"
  - Right: "Date: 4th Aug 24"

### Branding
- **Font:** Bold Sans-serif
- **Size:** 28-32px
- **Text:** "ScrollUp Today" or "ScrollUp\nToday"
- **Accent:** Orange dot (●) beside text

## 🔤 Headline Highlighting Rules

### What to Highlight
✅ **DO Highlight:**
- Key numbers ("32 killed", "56 followers")
- Important nouns ("Curfew", "Railway", "Districts")
- Action verbs ("Resume", "Killed", "Announced")
- Shocking/attention-grabbing words
- 2-4 word phrases maximum

❌ **DON'T Highlight:**
- Common words ("the", "a", "in", "to")
- Entire sentences
- More than 40% of headline
- Background information

### Examples:
```
❌ "Bangladesh Railway will resume railway operations"
✅ "Bangladesh Railway will resume RAILWAY OPERATIONS"
                                    ^red highlight^

❌ "32 killed in 13 districts in Bangladesh: Total Non-Cooperation"
✅ "32 KILLED in 13 DISTRICTS in Bangladesh: Total Non-Cooperation"
    ^red^      ^red^

❌ "An indefinite curfew from 6 PM today"
✅ "An INDEFINITE CURFEW from 6 PM today"
      ^red highlight^
```

## 📸 Photo Guidelines

### Photo Selection
✅ **Good Photos:**
- Clear, high-resolution
- Relevant to the story
- Strong focal point
- Good composition
- Recognizable subjects

❌ **Avoid:**
- Blurry or low-quality
- Unrelated stock photos
- Heavy text overlays already present
- Copyrighted without attribution

### Photo Placement
- **Bottom 55%** of frame
- **Slightly darkened** if text needs to overlay
- **No cropping** of important subjects
- **Maintain aspect ratio**

## 🎭 Brand Elements

### Logo/Branding Placement
**Position:** Bottom right corner  
**Components:**
1. Text: "ScrollUp Today"
2. Accent: Orange dot (●)

**On White/Black Backgrounds:**
- Use contrasting color
- No background needed
- Clear spacing from edges

**On Photos:**
- White text with subtle shadow
- Ensures readability
- Orange dot for brand consistency

## 📊 Caption Format

### Structure
```
[Engaging summary with emoji] 🚀

📰 Source: [News Source]
🕐 [Timestamp]

[Relevant hashtags]

Follow @scrl.up for latest Bangladesh news updates 🇧🇩

#ScrollUpToday #BangladeshNews #Category
```

### Example
```
BREAKING: Bangladesh Railway operations resume after 3-week halt! 🚂 
Goods trains to hit tracks tomorrow.

📰 Source: Jago News 24
🕐 August 11, 2024

#ScrollUpToday #BangladeshNews #Railway #Transport

Follow @scrl.up for latest Bangladesh news updates 🇧🇩
```

### Caption Guidelines
- ✅ Start with attention-grabbing statement
- ✅ Include emoji (1-2 max)
- ✅ Credit source
- ✅ Add timestamp
- ✅ 3-5 relevant hashtags
- ✅ End with call-to-action
- ❌ Don't exceed 2200 characters

## 🎯 Content Strategy

### Topics
1. **Breaking News** - Urgent, important updates
2. **Politics** - Government, policy, elections
3. **Social Issues** - Protests, movements, public sentiment
4. **Economy** - Business, trade, development
5. **Infrastructure** - Transport, utilities, facilities
6. **Culture** - Events, celebrations, traditions

### Posting Frequency
- **Target:** 5-10 posts per day
- **Peak Times:** 8-10 AM, 1-3 PM, 7-9 PM (Bangladesh time)
- **Consistency:** Regular schedule builds audience

### Quality Standards
✅ **Every Post Must:**
- Have clear, bold headline
- Credit original source
- Include timestamp
- Use brand colors correctly
- Show ScrollUp Today branding
- Be factually accurate

## 🚫 Brand Don'ts

❌ **Never:**
- Use different color schemes
- Remove source attribution
- Skip branding elements
- Use poor quality photos
- Highlight entire headlines
- Post without verification
- Use click-bait tactics
- Ignore brand guidelines

## ✅ Brand Do's

✅ **Always:**
- Maintain bold, clean aesthetic
- Use red highlights strategically
- Include ScrollUp Today branding
- Credit news sources properly
- Keep text readable
- Use high-quality images
- Stay true to news values
- Verify information

## 🎨 Design Variations

### Acceptable Variations
✅ **Can Change:**
- Background color (white ↔ black)
- Headline highlighting (which words)
- Photo vs. text-only style
- Text positioning (within guidelines)

❌ **Don't Change:**
- Brand colors (red, orange, black, white)
- Font style (bold, sans-serif)
- Branding placement (bottom right)
- Source/date format
- Overall clean aesthetic
